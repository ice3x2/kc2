## 1. Purpose

This document defines the KC2 physical layouts for the plateless low-profile no-stabilizer direction. The verified `kc2-x3` baseline remains a 77-key layout, while the active `kc2-x3-v2` target uses the fixed 71-key v4 layout selected from 1:1 visual previews and prior Choc V1 use experience.

Both variants split or replace every historical `>=2U` key so that no stabilizer is required. Duplicate legends such as `LShift`/`LShift`, `Enter`/`Enter`, and `Space`/`Space` are intentional physical-key decisions. Nonstandard keycaps may be 3D printed for the intended Choc V2 or MX assembly.

## 2. Policy

- Both no-stabilizer layouts use no `>=2U` physical keys, and `1.75U` is the largest permitted key width.
- The verified `kc2-x3` target retains its existing 77-key row extents and joined geometry.
- The active `kc2-x3-v2` target uses 71 keys and may reduce its PCB outline and joined geometry under `CON-ARCH-006`; the old `3.6 mm` inner-edge margin and fixed H1-H9 support pattern are not V2 invariants.
- V2 joined placement shall preserve nominal `19.05 mm` one-unit pitch where applicable and a nominal `1.00 mm` gap between `18.05 mm` keycap envelopes, within the tolerance defined by `CON-ARCH-006`.
- Joined placement and reduced bezel shall not violate copper-to-edge, socket, pad, routing, manufacturing, controller, antenna, USB, reset, or battery-lead clearances.
- The compact controller tab removes carrier-PCB battery solder pads, keeps no `BAT+`/`BAT-` routing on the carrier PCB, and targets a controller protrusion no wider than `54.0 mm` while preserving USB-C access, reset switch access, and antenna keepout.
- Firmware may map duplicate physical legends to identical or separate behaviors; the physical layout intentionally does not force that choice.

## 5. Split Decisions

| V2 area | Fixed v4 decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Left `Shift` | outer `1.25U LShift` + inner `1U LShift` | Keeps the larger target under the left pinky while remaining stabilizer-free. |
| Left bottom modifiers | `1.25U Ctrl`, `1.25U Win`, `1.25U Alt`, `1U Fn` | Preserves the accepted v4 preview. |
| Left `Space` | `1.25U Space` + `1.25U Space` | Preserves the accepted v4 preview. |
| Right top row | `1.25U BSPC` + `1U Del` | Replaces the former duplicate Backspace and removes the old outer Del column. |
| Right `Enter` | inner primary `1.5U Enter` + outer `1U Enter` | Matches the user's more comfortable Choc V1 experience. |
| Right shift row | `1U RShift` + `1U Up` + `1U Fn` | Moves Fn to the right of Up and removes PgDn. |
| Right bottom row | `1U B`, `1.75U Space`, `1.25U RAlt`, `1U RCtrl`, `1U Left`, `1U Down`, `1U Right` | Enlarges the main right Space without a stabilizer and fills the `8.75U` row. |
| Removed V2 keys | `Home`, `PgUp`, `PgDn`, one former right `Space`, and the former bottom-row right `Fn` position | Reduces the right half from 45 to 39 keys; Fn is retained beside Up. |

## 6. Implementation Notes

- The verified `kc2-x3` generator and firmware remain 77-key artifacts under `CON-ARCH-005`; they must not be silently converted to the V2 layout.
- The `kc2-x3-v2` generator, firmware transform, routing, housing, and fabrication manifests shall use the exact 71-key v4 map under `CON-ARCH-004`.
- Neither variant may create stabilizer markers, stabilizer footprints, or stabilizer keepouts.
- Right-side matrix column assignment must be regenerated and reviewed for the 39-key V2 half; naive row-array deletion is insufficient.
- The ZMK matrix and keymap should explicitly distinguish duplicate physical keys with stable physical IDs even when legends match.
- V2 joined visual and mechanical renders shall prove the keycap pitch and gap, concealed non-controller PCB outline, and the permitted controller/service exceptions defined by `CON-ARCH-006`.
- PCB regeneration, routing, firmware generation, housing generation, and DRC must be rerun after the V2 layout change. `CON-ARCH-004`, `CON-ARCH-006`, X-series preflight, fabrication-output inspection, deflection testing, and physical coupon/first-article evidence remain required before any orderable V2 claim.
- Compact controller generation must be verified by board-coordinate checks that prove `J_PWR1` and carrier battery nets are absent, reset still connects `RST` to `GND`, and the reduced tab preserves component and copper-edge clearance.
