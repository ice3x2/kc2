# KC2 X3 V2 1N4148W SOD-123 selection — 2026-09-01

## Decision

`CON-ARCH-004` now selects the exact Diodes Incorporated `1N4148W-13-F` in SOD-123 for all 70 X3 V2 matrix positions. It supersedes the temporary Jingdao `ES1B` / LCSC `C437840` selection.

The selected part is a flat molded SMD package suitable for tweezers and hand soldering. It is not the cylindrical MiniMELF/SOD-80 `LL4148`, the smaller SOD-323 `1N4148WS`, or the smaller SOD-523 `1N4148WT`.

## Controlled sources

- Diodes Incorporated product page: <https://www.diodes.com/part/view/1N4148W/>
- Diodes Incorporated data sheet `DS30086 Rev. 31-2`, September 2024: <https://www.diodes.com/datasheet/download/1N4148W.pdf>
- Eleparts exact orderable listing, goods `3417687`: <https://www.eleparts.co.kr/goods/view?no=3417687>

The manufacturer data sheet identifies `1N4148W-13-F` as a 10,000-piece tape-and-reel orderable part in SOD-123 and specifies a cathode band. The Eleparts page independently identifies manufacturer DIODES, full MPN `1N4148W-13-F`, SOD-123, 100 V and 300 mA. Supplier stock, MOQ, price and lead time are volatile and are not design dimensions or order approval.

## Electrical contract

| Field | Controlled value |
| --- | --- |
| Repetitive/DC reverse voltage | 100 V |
| Continuous forward current | 300 mA |
| Maximum forward voltage | 0.715 V at 1 mA; 0.855 V at 10 mA |
| Maximum total capacitance | 2 pF |
| Maximum reverse-recovery time | 4 ns |
| Pad 1 | Cathode, row net |
| Pad 2 | Anode, per-key switch net |

These guaranteed low-current voltage limits are materially stronger evidence for the keyboard scan operating range than extrapolating the former ES1B 1 A data. They do not replace the `CON-ARCH-004 AC-9` populated 3.0/3.3 V maximum-row and maximum-column scan test.

## Package and land contract

Diodes `DS30086 Rev. 31-2` specifies:

- maximum body length `2.85 mm`;
- maximum body width `1.70 mm`;
- maximum terminal span `3.85 mm`;
- maximum height `1.35 mm`;
- suggested pad `X=0.90 mm`, `Y=0.95 mm`, total span `X1=4.05 mm`.

KC2 deliberately uses a larger hand-solder land:

- two `1.40 mm x 1.55 mm` round-rectangle pads;
- centers at `-1.80 mm` and `+1.80 mm`;
- `2.20 mm` inner gap and `5.00 mm` total copper span;
- pad 1 cathode marking visible in the mirrored bottom-assembly view.

The land extends the official suggestion by `0.50 mm` in pad length, `0.60 mm` in pad width and `0.95 mm` in total span. The maximum `3.85 mm` terminal span still overlaps both pads. The implementation and documentation must call this a KC2 hand-solder land, not a manufacturer-recommended land.

## Mechanical consequences

The housing model uses the official `1.35 mm` height plus a separate `0.30 mm` solder-fillet allowance. Against the nominal `2.50 mm` structural plate, this leaves `0.85 mm` to the exterior plate bottom and `1.85 mm` to the existing Z=`-1.00 mm` desk datum before print tolerance. The desk datum remains unchanged because it is shared by all contacts and by the provisional battery-stack clearance.

The smaller package and land reopen every diode placement, M1.4 clamp, housing cutout, support, DRC, fabrication, mechanical, render, coupon and hash-binding claim. The active P3 design keeps 8 left / 9 right M1.4 clamps, moves only clearance-limited centers, and widens the one-per-key housing desk contacts from `2.00 mm` to `2.40 mm`; it does not treat extra PCB holes as typing-load supports.

## Order gate

The PCB and housing remain `order_ready=false`. A generic `1N4148W` listing is not enough to approve a substitution because manufacturer dimensions, marking and order identity can differ. If JLCPCB does not offer the exact controlled part, the quote package must omit the diode population and identify hand assembly instead of silently selecting another manufacturer.
